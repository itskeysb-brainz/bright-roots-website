Spaces made for little explorers.

Spaces made for little explorers

Every corner of Bright Roots is designed to be safe, colourful and full of things to discover.

Splash & water play

Our splash pool is a daily favourite, with shallow water, floats and colourful toys and caring adults right beside the children.

Supervised, gentle water funBuilds balance and courage

Sandpit

A little laboratory for scooping, pouring, digging and building, with child-sized diggers and buckets for imaginative play.

Hands-on maths and scienceTeamwork and sharing

Playground & play houses

Slides, play houses and open grass give children room to run, climb and dream, every single day.

Climbing and slidingFresh air and sunshine

Cosy rest room

A bright, clean room with colourful beds and calm routines for afternoon naps and overnight stays.

Calm, familiar routinesCaring adults close by

Shady garden & lawn

Our shaded garden is the heart of cultural days, music, dance and storytime under the trees.

Music, drumming and danceSpace for celebrations

School van

Our branded Bright Roots van brings children to and from school with careful pick-ups and drop-offs.

A familiar, friendly ridePeace of mind for parents
