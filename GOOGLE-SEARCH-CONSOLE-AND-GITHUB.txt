GITHUB PUBLISHING
1. Extract bright-roots-daycare.zip on your computer.
2. Open the extracted folder and confirm index.html is visible immediately.
3. In the GitHub repository, upload the CONTENTS of this folder (not an extra enclosing folder).
4. Confirm index.html appears at the top level of the repository.
5. Configure GitHub Pages or deploy through your selected host.
6. Test the live homepage and several inner pages before submitting to Google.

GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (FREE)
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/ and sign in using the Google account the school controls.
2. Click Add property.
3. Choose Domain and enter: brightrootsdaycareandkindergarten.com
   Do not include https:// or www.
4. Google will show a unique DNS TXT verification record. Copy the exact value.
5. Sign in to the ACTIVE DNS provider for the domain. The registrar may be Truehost while DNS is managed elsewhere (for example Cloudflare). Add a TXT record:
   Type: TXT
   Name/Host: @ (or blank if the DNS provider requires blank)
   Value/Content: paste the exact Google verification string
   TTL: Auto/default
6. Do not remove or change existing nameserver, MX, email, or website records. Add only Google's TXT record.
7. Return to Search Console and click Verify. DNS changes may take time to appear.
8. After verification, open Sitemaps in the left menu and submit:
   sitemap.xml
9. Use URL Inspection to test the homepage:
   https://brightrootsdaycareandkindergarten.com/
   If it is live and accessible, choose Request Indexing.
10. Repeat URL Inspection for the main Admissions, Programmes and Contact pages if needed.

IMPORTANT SEO EXPECTATIONS
- Search Console submission does not guarantee instant indexing or a first-place ranking.
- Google decides whether and where pages appear. Ranking depends on relevance, quality, competition, reputation, links, and other signals.
- Keep the school name, address/location, phone numbers, opening/admissions information and services accurate and consistent.
- Ask the school to create/claim and verify its Google Business Profile, then keep its name, address, phone, website and hours accurate. This is especially important for local searches.
- Do not add fake reviews, fake addresses, or repeated keyword stuffing.
- The site needs to be publicly live over HTTPS before Google can crawl it reliably.

VERIFICATION TOKEN
No Google verification token has been inserted in this package. Google generates a private, account-specific token after you add the property. Add that exact token in the DNS provider; never use a token copied from another website.
