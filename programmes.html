Our youngest children

Daycare

A warm, gentle start.

Our daycare is for our youngest children and for families who need dependable care through the working day. Little ones settle into calm, familiar routines with caring adults who offer plenty of cuddles, songs, sensory play and time to rest.

We follow each child’s own pace for playing, resting and eating, and we stay in close touch with parents so handing over your child always feels easy and safe.

  • Calm, familiar daily routines
  • Sensory play, songs and stories
  • Cosy rest times in a quiet room
  • Settling-in at your child’s own pace
  • Nightcare available for families who need it

Little Learners

First friends, first words.

Little Learners is where children begin to explore the world on their own two feet. Days are full of songs, rhymes, picture books and hands-on play that grow vocabulary, movement and independence.

Children discover how to share, take turns and say what they need, while caregivers gently guide everyday habits such as washing hands, feeding themselves and tidying up.

  • Language through songs, rhymes and stories
  • Sandpit and water play for senses and coordination
  • First social skills: sharing and turn-taking
  • Independence built in small, happy steps

About 3 to 4 years

Growing Learners

Curious minds in motion.

Growing Learners are full of questions, and we love every one of them. Play becomes richer: building, pretend games, art, music and outdoor challenges introduce early counting, shapes, colours, letters and sounds.

Our water and sand play double as little science lessons, where children test ideas such as full and empty, sink and float, and learn to explain what they discover.

  • Early number, shape and letter awareness through play
  • Art, music and imaginative games
  • Group activities that build teamwork
  • Outdoor adventure on slides and play houses

About 4 to 5 years

Ready Learners

Confident and ready for big school.

Ready Learners are preparing for the next big step. Through structured play and stories, children strengthen listening, pre-writing, number sense and early reading skills, along with the confidence to speak up and join in.

Cultural days and group projects help them feel proud of who they are and respectful of others, so they walk into primary school with a steady voice and a smile.

  • Pre-writing and early reading foundations
  • Number sense and simple problem solving
  • Listening, speaking and showing confidence
  • Music, culture and celebration
  • A smooth, happy move to primary school
