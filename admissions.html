Admissions & Enrolment 2027

We are now enrolling children for the 2027 school year. Whether you need Daycare for a little one, a place in Little Learners or Growing Learners, or a confident start for Ready Learners, we would love to welcome your family.

The best first step is a visit. Come and see our compound, meet the team and tell us about your child. Places and start dates are confirmed when you enquire.

How to join, in four easy steps

Enquire

Call, WhatsApp or use the form below and tell us a little about your child.

Visit us

Tour the compound, meet our team and ask every question you have.

Register

Complete the registration form and share your child’s details and documents.

Settle in

We plan a gentle first week together so your child feels safe and happy from day one.

What to prepare

These are the things families usually share when registering. We will confirm the exact list with you when you enquire.

  • Your child’s birth certificate or other proof of age
  • Immunisation or health card
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Emergency contacts and who is allowed to collect your child
  • Any allergies, medical needs or care notes we should know
  • Recent passport-size photos of your child

Admissions questions

When can my child start?

Start dates and available places are confirmed when you enquire. Contact us early so we can reserve the right place for 2027 and plan a gentle start.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Yes, and we encourage it. Call or WhatsApp us to arrange a visit so you can tour the compound and meet the team.

Do you offer nightcare?

Yes. We offer Day & Nightcare for families who need dependable care beyond the school day. Ask us about it when you enquire.

How do I get the 2027 fees?

Message us on WhatsApp or visit our Fees & Info page. We share the current fee details clearly with every enquiring family.

Is school transport available?

We have a branded Bright Roots van. Ask us whether your area can be served and how pick-ups and drop-offs work.
