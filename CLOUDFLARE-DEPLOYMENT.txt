No framework or build step is required.

After deployment:
1. Add brightrootsdaycareandkindergarten.com under Workers & Pages > your Pages project > Custom domains.

4. Test the homepage, inner pages, images, video and HTTPS.
5. Submit https://brightrootsdaycareandkindergarten.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
